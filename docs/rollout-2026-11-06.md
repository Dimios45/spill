## What this establishes—and what it does not

The harness successfully progressed through repeated Dream chunks, obtained fresh
Astra assessments, honored pause/resume and used Karma's home/shutdown path.
It did not establish a completed pill transfer.

Before the pause, Astra described offset fingers near the rims and unconfirmed
grasp security. After Resume it interpreted the new views as rim engagement and
proposed a cautious lift. These are model judgments, not independent physical
measurements. The records do not prove whether the difference came from scene
changes, viewpoint/occlusion or inconsistent interpretation. No manual scene
intervention should be inferred merely from the Resume event.
